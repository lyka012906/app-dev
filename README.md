# She's Dating the Gangster 

## A. Introduction
She's Dating the Gangster is a popular romantic comedy and drama film released in 2014. The movie tells the story of two high school students who pretend to be in a relationship to make an ex-girlfriend jealous. Over time, their fake relationship turns into real love as they face life challenges together. It is a heartwarming story that explores how young love can deeply affect a person's life.
---
## B. Synopsis
Athena Dizon plays a trick on the school bad boy, Kenji Delos Reyes, which leads to an unexpected deal between them. Kenji forces Athena to pretend to be his girlfriend so his ex-girlfriend Abigail will feel jealous. Athena dislikes Kenji at first because he is loud and hotheaded, but she slowly sees his caring side. As they spend more time together, they fall in love and make a promise to stay together. Years later, their family members try to reconnect them after many years of separation.
> "Great love stories deserve a second chance."
---
### C. Character
1. *Athena Dizon* - A kind and hardworking high school girl who agrees to pretend to date Kenji.
2. *Kenji Delos Reyes* - A hotheaded school gangster who secretly has a soft heart for the people he cares about.
3. *Athena Abigail Tizon* - Kenji's former girlfriend who suffers from a serious illness.
4. *Kenneth Delos Reyes* - Kenji's son in the present time who searches for his father's lost love.
5. *Kelay Dizon* - Athena's niece who helps Kenneth discover the truth about their parents' past.
---
### D. Personal Review 
I chose this movie because it presents a sweet and emotional story about true love.
